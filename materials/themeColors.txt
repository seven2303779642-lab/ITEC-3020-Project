USAGE NOTES

Use black or warm porcelain for most large surfaces.
Use wine magenta for primary buttons, links, and important headings.
Use antique gold for small labels, focus states, borders, and status details.
Use blush pink for subtle backgrounds and hover shadows.
Use purple and icy blue-grey sparingly as secondary highlights.
Do not make large sections entirely pink or gold.
The dark-theme switching logic will be added in the future.
